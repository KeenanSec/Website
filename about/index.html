About

Keenan Morrison

I'm a cybersecurity student focused on DFIR, detection engineering, and cloud security. Most of my learning happens hands-on: building and breaking a home SOC lab, then writing up what the evidence actually showed.

My home lab runs a hardened Wazuh SIEM with Sysmon telemetry, an Active Directory domain, and OPNsense-segmented networks, so I can generate real detections instead of reading about them. From there I work incidents end to end: triaging Windows artifacts with KAPE, pulling memory apart in Volatility 3, and tracing activity through packet captures. Those investigations become the writeups on my blog.

I rank in the Top 1% on TryHackMe and stay active on Hack The Box. My near-term goal is a SOC analyst or incident response role where I can do this work full time.
